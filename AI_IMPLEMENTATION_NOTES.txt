SoutouGhast AI implementation summary

Implemented in source:
- Distance-keeping combat movement
- Above/behind flanking behavior
- 3D orbiting with obstacle-aware reposition attempts
- Move-to-last-seen-position memory when line of sight is lost
- Combat phases: scouting, chasing, bombardment, enraged, retreating
- Predictive aiming
- Feint movement
- Temperament / emotion profiles: cowardly, tenacious, irascible, balanced
- Terrain usage: openness-aware altitude/range and lava-aggression bias
- Fireball variants:
  - FAST_SMALL
  - HEAVY_SLOW
  - GROUND_FIRE
  - SPLITTER
  - WALL_BURST
