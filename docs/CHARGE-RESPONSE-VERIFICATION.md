## Limits and feedback

This proves native-position observation, production charge-adapter wiring, bounded learned preference and actual emission in one controlled scenario. Core tests cover constant strafe, baseline/thresholds, LOS/target/time gaps,600tick expiry and unsuccessful emission; those negative/expiry cases are not native trials. Client identity does not establish rendered cues. Natural human reactions, ordinary rally, unobserved natural selections, late connection, arbitrary unload/crash and full release remain separate gates.

Tank improvement: distinguish observed response timing from intent, native navigation from scripted positions, controlled fixture actors from canonical combat, and pure counterfactual selector calculations from actual emission. Retain this in AF0047/guide. A future bounded consumer actor recipe may reduce duplicate probe setup; it needs demonstrated consumers and must not become a generic override API or second canonical evidence store.
